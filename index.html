Who Are We?

NovaMed Pharmaceuticals is a name of trust, excellence and quality in the pharmaceutical industry of Pakistan. We strive to serve the humanity in the best possible way by providing them a wide range of innovative and quality medicines. We are manufacturing the products of the big national and multinational companies of Pakistan, in diverse dosage forms, across many therapeutic classes. These products are available in Pakistan and some of the export markets. NovaMed has been rated ‘A’ by the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan for cGMP compliance and is also ISO 9001: 2008 certified.
