Company Overview

We continously invest in upgrading our facility and information system which enables us to enhance our business avenues and implement effective controls to further improve the efficiency and productivity of our organisation. Novamed has dedicated Qualtiy Contorl and Qualtiy Assurance Departments. Both work hand in hand to achieve quality compliance goals. We believe in providing our customers with safe and effective medicine and are committed to encouraging happier and healthier lives across the globe by actively pursuing and engaging in the best innovative practiecs for the provision of quality pharmaceutical and healthcare products.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop and manufacture high-quality generic drugs that will improve the quality of life of our customers. With a result-oriented culture, we aspire to create a conducive work environment which promotes the professional development of our employees so that they may become well-rounded individuals in all aspects of life. We achieve our goals by staying true to our core values: integrity, good ethics, simplicity and dedication to customer satisfaction.

Our Vision
